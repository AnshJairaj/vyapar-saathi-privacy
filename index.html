About Vyapar Saathi

Vyapar Saathi is a business management application designed to help shopkeepers manage their daily business activities in one convenient place.

Features

  • Record daily sales and generate bills.
  • Manage stock and inventory.
  • Track customer dues and payments.
  • Maintain customer transaction history.
  • Back up and restore business data.
